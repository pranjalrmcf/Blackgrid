# Current-system repairs v2 — development protocol

The 24 v1 scenarios and their results have been inspected. They are regression/development evidence, not fresh held-out evaluation. Preserve all v1 artifacts and unresolved billing records. This repair uses offline checks only; no paid calls or renewed allowance.

Acceptance criteria frozen before implementation:

- Community-versus-baseline questions mentioning contact counts route to community analysis; explicit centrality and bridge questions retain their targets.
- Reject pair-specific calibrated real forecast probabilities and transfers of synthetic posteriors to real community probabilities, before numerical tool execution, with a useful explicit reason. A forced family cannot bypass this boundary. Ordinary synthetic posterior/calibration reviews remain supported.
- Saved synthetic experiment decisions report registered cost, net value and stop rationale. Causal reviews state the estimand, treatment/outcome/unit, adjustment set and supplied assumptions, without claiming their empirical verification or exposing simulator truth.
- Validated forecast summaries use the existing evidence-bound local vocabulary and the existing forecast critic, without an extra interpretation model request. Tool-only comparison retains its LLM writer. Invalid forecast claims remain rejected in direct critic checks. Missing tools must remain partial; numerical settings and ranking semantics do not change.
- Preserve useful planner refusal explanations in reports, clearly attributing untrusted planner wording. No unsupported numbers are promoted to verified findings.
- Focused offline regression checks and the existing suite must pass. Record source modifications and scientific limits; a new live pass rate requires a separately versioned evaluation and resolution of unknown billing.
